Questions

Before you start

What happens to my files and generations if I cancel?+

Everything stays exportable for 30 days after cancellation. You can download every chat, image, video, and document from your workspace at any time before then.

Does the identity lock work across image and video together?+

Yes. Once a face, wardrobe, or style is locked, it carries across the Image and Video modules until you unlock or swap it — so a character stays consistent across a whole campaign.

Can I bring my own storage or connect existing tools?+

Yes. Google Drive, Dropbox, Notion, GitHub, Slack, and calendar connections are available from Settings, and workspace data syncs automatically once connected.

Is voice cloning available to everyone?+

Voice cloning requires identity verification and explicit consent from the voice's owner before it can be used, in line with our safety policy.
